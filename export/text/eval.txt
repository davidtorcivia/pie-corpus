h₃rḗḱs deywós-kʷe h₃rḗḱs h₁est; só n̥putlós. h₃rḗḱs súHnum wl̥nh₁to. tósyo ǵʰéwtorm̥ prēḱst: "súHnus moy ǵn̥h₁yetōd!" ǵʰéwtōr tom h₃rḗǵm̥ wewked: "h₁yáǵeswo deywóm wérunom". úpo h₃rḗḱs deywóm wérunom sesole nú deywóm h₁yaǵeto. "ḱludʰí moi, pter werune!" deywós wérunos diwés km̥tá gʷah₂t. "kʷíd wēlh₁si?" "súHnum wēlh₁mi." "tód h₁estu", wéwked lewkós deywós wérunos. nu h₃réḱs pótnih₂ súHnum ǵeǵonh₁e.

to rḗḱs éh₁est. so n̥putlos éh₁est. so rḗḱs súHnum éwel(e)t. "kʷíd welsi?" "wélmi súHnum." "tód h₁éstu", wéukʷet loukós deiwos werunos.

só nu km̥ta diwós gʷāt.

h₂ówis h₁éḱwōs-kʷe gʷr̥Hḗi h₂ówis, kʷésyo wlh₂néH ne (h₁é) est, h₁éḱwons spéḱet, h₁oinom gʰe gʷr̥Húm wóǵʰom wéǵʰontm̥ h₁oinom-kʷe méǵHm bʰórom, h₁oinom-kʷe ǵʰménm̥ Hṓḱu bʰérontm̥. h₂ówis tu h₁eḱwoibʰ(y)os weukʷét, "ḱḗr Hegʰnutór moi h₁éḱwons Héǵontm̥ Hnérm̥ widn̥tbʰ(y)os." h₁éḱwōs tu weukʷónt, "ḱludʰí, h₂ówei, ḱḗr gʰe Hegʰnutór n̥sméi widn̥tbʰ(y)ós. Hnḗr, pótis, h₂éwyom r̥ wl̥h₂néHm sebʰi kʷr̥néuti nu gʷʰérmom wéstrom néǵʰi h₂éwyom wl̥h₂néH h₁ésti." tód ḱeḱluwṓs h₂ówis Héǵrom bʰugét.

h₂ówis h₁éḱwōs-kʷe h₂áwey h₁yosméy h₂wl̥h₁náh₂ né h₁ést, só h₁éḱwoms derḱt. só gʷr̥Húm wóǵʰom weǵʰed; só méǵh₂m̥ bʰórom; só dʰǵʰémonm̥ h₂ṓḱu bʰered. h₂ówis h₁ékʷoybʰyos wewked: "dʰǵʰémonm̥ spéḱyoh₂ h₁éḱwoms-kʷe h₂áǵeti, ḱḗr moy agʰnutor". h₁éḱwōs tu wewkond: "ḱludʰí, h₂owey! tód spéḱyomes, n̥sméy agʰnutór ḱḗr: dʰǵʰémō, pótis, sē h₂áwyes h₂wl̥h₁náh₂ gʷʰérmom wéstrom wept, h₂áwibʰyos tu h₂wl̥h₁náh₂ né h₁esti. tód ḱeḱluwṓs h₂ówis h₂aǵróm bʰuged.

owis eḱwoi kʷe owis, yesmin wl̥nā ne ēst, dedorḱe eḱwons wogʰom gʷr̥um wegʰontn̥s, bʰorom meǵHm, monum ōḱu bʰerontn̥s. owis eḱwobʰos eweukʷet, "ḱerd agʰnutai moi widn̥tei ǵʰm̥onm̥ eḱwons aǵontm̥". eḱwoi eweukʷont, "ḱludʰi, owi, ḱerd agʰnutai dedr̥ḱusbʰos: monus potis wl̥nām owiōm temneti: sebʰei gʷʰermom westrom, owibʰos kʷe wl̥nā ne esti." tod ḱeḱluwōs owis aǵrom ebʰuget.

owis eḱwōses-kʷe tod ḱeḱruwos owis aǵrom ebʰuget.

owis eḱwōskʷe gʷHrēi owis, kʷesyo wl̥Hnā ne ēst, eḱwōns espeḱet, oinom gʰe gʷr̥um woǵʰom weǵʰontm̥, oinomkʷe meǵam bʰorom, oinomkʷe ǵʰm̥enm̥ ōḱu bʰerontm̥. owis nu eḱwobʰ(y)os (eḱwomos) ewewkʷet, "ḱēr agʰnutoi moi eḱwōns aǵontm̥ nerm̥ widn̥tei". eḱwōs tu ewewkʷont, "ḱludʰi, owei, ḱēr gʰe agʰnutoi n̥smei widn̥tbʰ(y)os (widn̥tmos): nēr, potis, owiōm r̥ wl̥Hnām sebʰi gʷʰermom westrom kʷrn̥euti. neǵʰi owiōm wl̥Hnā esti." tod ḱeḱluwōs owis aǵrom ebʰuget.

h₂ówis h₁éḱwōskʷe h₂ówis, (H)yésmin h₂wlh₂néh₂ ne éh₁est, dedorḱe (h₁)éḱwons, tóm, wóǵʰom gʷérh₂um wéǵʰontm, tóm, bʰórom méǵoh₂m, tóm, dʰǵʰémonm h₂oHḱú bʰérontm. h₂ówis (h₁)éḱwobʰos ewewkʷe(t), "ḱḗrd h₂gʰnutoy moy widntéy dʰǵʰmónm (h₁)éḱwons h₂éǵontm." (h₁)éḱwōs ewewkʷ, "ḱludʰí, h₂ówi! ḱḗrd h₂gʰnutoy widntbʰós: dʰǵʰémō(n), pótis, h₂wlnéh₂m h₂ówyom kʷnewti sébʰoy gʷʰérmom wéstrom; h₂éwibʰoskʷe h₂wlh₂néh₂ né h₁esti." tód ḱeḱluwṓs h₂ówis h₂éǵrom ebʰuge(t).

awis akwāsas ka. awis, yasmin warnā na ā ast, dadarka akwams, tam, wāgʰam garum wagʰantam, tam, bʰāram magʰam, tam, manum āku bʰarantam. awis akwabʰyams ā wawakat: kard agʰnutai mai widanti manum akwams agantam. akwāsas ā wawakant: krudʰi awai, kard agʰnutai wiwidwant- swas: manus patis warnām awisāms karnauti swabʰyam gʰarmam wastram awibʰyams ka warnā na asti. tat kukruwants awis agram ā bʰugat.

owis eḱwōs kʷe owis, yāi wl̥nā ne eest, dedorḱe eḱwons, tom woǵʰom gʷr̥um weǵʰontm̥, tom bʰorom meǵm̥, tom ǵʰm̥onm̥ ōku bʰerontm̥. owis eḱwobʰyos eweket, "ḱerd angʰetai moi widontei ǵʰm̥onm̥ eḱwons aǵontm̥." eḱwos wewekur, "ḱludʰe, owei! ḱerd angʰetai widontbʰyos: ǵʰm̥on, potis, wl̥nam owiyōm kʷr̥neti soi gʷʰermom westrom; owibʰyos kʷe wl̥nā ne esti". tod ḱeḱlōts owis aǵrom ebʰuget.
