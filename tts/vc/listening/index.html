Edit distance uses a wav2vec2 phone recognizer (xlsr-53-espeak-cv-ft) on the source and on each clip, divided by the source length. The recognizer never outputs χ, ʔ or murmured stops, so it measures how stable the heard phone string is, not whether the PIE sounds are right. The control rows change nothing phonetic: the source after the page's AAC encoding, the same eSpeak codes rendered at a higher pitch, and the source pitch-shifted by a phase vocoder. They show how much the recognizer moves on its own; on 1-second words that is a lot, so the hymn-line column and the logit DTW distance (frame-level, continuous) are the steadier guides. χ noise kept is the length of voiceless noise before voicing in the χ-initial items (h₂éwsos, h₂ówis, h₂éǵeti, the first hymn line), clip over source. Accent contrast kept is the semitone gap between the source's high-tone frames and the rest, in the clip, divided by the same gap in the source. Green, amber and red mark distance within 0.15, 0.35 or more of the eSpeak-pitch control, and F0 r of at least 0.7, 0.4 or less.
