SCI-04 — State what the decoy control measures
Classification: interpretation correction now; stronger control construction is a future design change.
Real candidates require at least two shared root groups, including one outside the 50 most frequent. Decoys allow at most one shared root and no rare-root overlap. Stage C-S retains this mechanism, with its additional corpus/class restrictions.
This comparison can show enrichment of reader claims under targeted retrieval versus the specified decoys. It does not directly estimate historical false discoveries among equally plausible, root-matched candidates.
The difficult negatives are ordinary independently formed phrases, incongruent constructions, late translation/borrowing, or wrong mappings that nevertheless satisfy the retrieval rule.
There is also a claim-attribution problem: readers may compare across bundles, and decoys retain genuine pivots. In mixed claims C0001 and C0005, a cited bundle can contribute only its unchanged Celtic pivot. Decoy-attributed claim weight therefore does not necessarily represent a false relationship involving a replaced decoy partner.
Immediate correction
Qualify direct historical “false-discovery rate” language in the preregistration explanations, design notes and result interpretations. Keep the historical design record intact and attach a dated clarification rather than rewriting what was preregistered.
Do not call every decoy-attributed claim a false historical discovery.
Proposed next experiment
Consider a small, separately preregistered control arm with:
- Real hard-negative passages matched on relevant root overlap, retrieval score, genre, length and source characteristics.
- Independent justification for their negative status: generic, borrowed, translated, or structurally unrelated.
- Blinded known-positive inherited comparisons.
- Explicit relationship-level eligibility, weighting and stopping rules.
This proposal is not an incidental code fix. Register it before new reading and keep its results distinguishable from the original control arm.
Sources: decoy rule, Stage C-S wrapper, preregistration and amendments, design proposal.
