# Knee/sleep research (KNEE as bodily support and SLEEP/DREAM)

David's own research, done over 49 rounds with another AI tool. This directory holds a curated, readable subset; the bulk stays outside the repo.

## What the packet claims (from its own summary, R49)
- It is a source-controlled historical proposal, not a demonstrated Proto-Indo-European convention.
- Anchor: Hittite Nerik KUB 36.89 rev. 56'-59' and Greek Anthologia Palatina 7.170 pair inherited knee vocabulary and *swep-family sleep vocabulary with a person sleeping while supported on knees.
- That warrants proposing an inherited lexical-semantic association KNEE[SUPPORT]-SLEEP/DREAM; no PIE sentence, shared metre or probability is recovered.
- Unresolved: whether the pairing was conventional before the languages split, or inherited knee-support usage and sleep diction were combined locally in each tradition.
- Later Persian, Irish, Norse and Latin scenes widen the range, but copies and translations are one transmission, not independent branches; false positives (Welsh elbows, Druimenach, Jataka-stava) were removed.
- Rounds 37-49 added controls (Hittite knee placement without sleep, Iliad 22.500-504, restorations, CTH 372-374 dream diction), not a new independent early branch.
- No priority or peer-review claim is made.
