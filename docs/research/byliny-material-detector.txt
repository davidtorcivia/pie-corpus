Method: classify before normalization/mapping, using OCR paragraph boundaries and
a maximum nine-line window. Wrapped prose needs scholarly vocabulary, several
long lines, line-end word breaks, internal sentence punctuation and a vowel-count
proxy. Separate rules cover recording/editorial markers and signed footnotes.
Notes headings stop any Wikisource page until the next page. Original IDs and
source text are preserved. Quoted passages and primary prose without editorial
evidence stay. Modern/pre-reform orthography and inline footnote calls alone do
not cause rejection. No claims of stress-based metrical scanning.
