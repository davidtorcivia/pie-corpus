# Literature review: Phase 8c candidates and related findings (2026-10-23)

Data: `data/research/literature/bibliography.json` (CSL-JSON, 68 entries) and `data/research/literature/findings.jsonl` (31 findings, `lit-0001` to `lit-0031`). Every finding cites a bibliography id and a locator. This document summarises them. Quotations are kept to a few words.

The Phase 8c novelty check (`docs/research/8c-novelty.md`, `data/research/8c/stageB/review/novelty-{1,2}.jsonl`) used only the books held locally. This review checks the three candidates, plus 'immortal horses' (Stage A C048), against the journal and monograph literature that could be reached legitimately online. No shadow libraries were used. Copyrighted books that are online only as user uploads were not read.

### 'Eat raw flesh': what is known, what is new
- **Known: the compound.** The equation ὠμηστής ~ āmā́d- appears already in Murray (1907; the 1924 printing was read) and in Frisk's GEW. West p. 80 gives it with RV 10.87.7 and AV 11.10.8.
- **Known: the collocation.** Massetti 2019 §115 argues that the compound rests on an **inherited collocation [raw (carrion flesh) - eat]** (*Hōm-ó- + *h₁ed-). She cites Il. 4.35 ὠμὸν βεβρώθοις, with the verb renewed, and substitutes in other branches: YAv. kərəfš.xvara- (V. 6.45) and ON Hræsvelgr.
- **Known: the vulture verse.** The Vedic verse behind the 8c 'scene match' is AV 11.10.8 (the 8c files call it "AVŚ 11.10.18"). West already cites it as evidence for the compound.
- **Not found, so new:** the free clause with all three cognates in one line, AV 8.6.23ab (cited in 8c as "8.6.48") ~ Il. 22.347 ὤμ' … κρέα ἔδμεναι.
- **Not found, so new:** the noun phrase with the noun *kréwh₂-, āmásya kravíṣaḥ (RV 1.162.10b) ~ ὠμὰ κρέα.
- **Not found, so new:** the explicit comparison of the corpse-tearing scenes (AV 11.10.8, RV 10.87.7 ~ Il. 11.454, 22.67).
- **Not found, so new:** the ON witness hrátt + eta.
- Stage A called this 'partly known: compound only'. The literature now says 'partly known: the compound and the underlying collocation are both proposed'. Any write-up must cite Massetti 2019 §115.

### 'Clothe in the immortal': what is known, what is new
- **Known: Greek and the word equations.** ἄμβροτα εἵματα of Sarpedon's corpse (West p. 157 n. 126; Garcia 2013; Roscher 1883: 59 reads it as embalming). The equations ἄμβροτος = amŕ̥ta- and εἷμα/ἕννυμι = vas-.
- **Known: a Vedic *wes- garment set beside one Greek anchor.** Kölligan 2007 p. 115 n. 22 puts the 8c anchor h.Hom. 6.6 (περὶ δ' ἄμβροτα εἵματα ἕσσαν) next to Uṣas's shining garment (RV 7.77.2 rúśad vā́saḥ). The garment word is cognate there, the adjective is not.
- **Known: the well-clothed goddess.** Massetti 2019 §51 treats this inherited image, crediting Durante 1976: 95f.
- **Not found:** the collocation amŕ̥ta- + vas- (RV 6.75.18 = AV 7.118.1 amŕ̥tena … vastām; AV 9.1.1cd amṛ́taṃ vásānām, cited in 8c as "9.1.2") set beside the Greek. Whitney (vol. 1 p. 470) and Jamison's commentary give only Vedic parallels.
- **Caution: the contexts differ.** Homeric ἄμβροτα εἵματα otherwise dresses the dead (Il. 16.670, 680; Od. 24.59; anonymous CAMWS 2022 abstract, lit-0024). RV 6.75.18 is an armour charm for a living warrior. The 8c review called the context 'close'. It is closer to 'a body made inviolable' than to a shared scene.

### 'Hear my word': what is known, what is new
- **Known: 'hear me'.** *ḱludʰí moi* is a textbook formula (West, Schmitt, Matasović 2018).
- **Not found:** *ḱlew- with the cognate object vácas- = ἔπος. It is absent from Massetti 2019's full collocation index and from Muellner 1976. Jamison's commentary on RV 1.145.3 and 10.66.11 has no comparison.
- **Caution 1.** García Ramón (2017 abstract) counts [say/hear words] as the *unmarked* pattern. Against it he sets metaphorical patterns such as [pour/drink words]. Massetti 2025 p. 44 quotes RV 3.33.10 (the rivers to the poet, śr̥ṇavāmā vácāṃsi), a non-prayer use. The pairing may therefore be ordinary language. Its weight rests on the exact s-stem *wékʷos and on the prayer setting.
- **Caution 2.** Il. 14.234 is Hera's request to Hypnos, not a prayer: Muellner calls it the secular counterpart of the prayer line Il. 16.236. The Greek prayer witness is Il. 16.236 alone.

### 'Immortal horses': what is known, what is new
- The parallel West credits to Durante 1976: 55 is Greek gods' horses fed ambrosia ~ Mithra's steeds (Yt 10.125). In TITUS's text of Yt 10.125 the Avestan adjective is **anaoša-** 'undying', not the cognate aməša-. The recorded parallel is therefore a motif with a lexical substitute.
- The Vedic verse is **AV 13.1.24a** (cited in 8c as "13.1.47"). West p. 205 cites AV 13.1.24, but only for the plural of the Sun's horses. So the verse is known, and the pair amŕ̥tāḥ … áśvāḥ ~ ἵπποι ἄμβροτοι (Il. 16.381) is not stated in anything read.
- Durante may cite the Vedic as well. Until he is checked, the verdict is 'probably new as a lexical pair, motif known'.

**Lexicon**
- 'raw' is *h₂eh₃mós in the lexicon. The 8c claims also write *h₂oh₃mó-, and Massetti writes *(H)ōm-ó-. Record the alternatives and normalise the spelling in write-ups (lit-0025).

**Methods (8a/8c)**
- **Closest prior computational work.** Brigada Villa, Biagetti, Ginevra & Zanchi 2023 pair the Sanskrit WordNet with the Vedic Treebank to extract formulas; their project compares Vedic and Homeric constructions. Ginevra et al. 2024 apply a treebank approach to Old English. McGillivray & Rodda 2024 measure Homeric formularity with valency lexica. None does cross-branch cognate co-occurrence as 8a does (lit-0019 to lit-0021).
- **Review rubric.** Finkelberg 1986 and Volk 2002 (the κλέος ἄφθιτον debate) show that a cognate match does not by itself establish an inherited formula. Add a Greek-internal formularity check to the 8c rubric (lit-0022).

## 6. How to cite these findings
- Cite the primary work and locator from `findings.jsonl` (for example "Massetti 2019: 110-112, §115"), not this file.
- For the project's own observations, cite "PIE corpus project, Phase 8c (2026), docs/research/8c-results.md" together with this review's id (for example lit-0006) and the access date 2026-10-23.
- State the candidates as additions to known formulas, give the corrected AV verse numbers (section 2), and say that Durante 1976 has not been checked. Until an Indo-Europeanist confirms them, they remain candidates.
